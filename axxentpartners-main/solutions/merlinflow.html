Merlin: Prism Managed Wallets in Action

Merlin is a specialized facet of Prism, delivered via the Federated Value Platform (FVP). It transforms blocked or complex transactions into seamless, compliant checkouts—routing payments through stablecoins, fiat ramps, or programmable settlements while handling compliance and customer education in the background.

The result: merchants recover sales that would otherwise fail, and consumers complete purchases with one-click confidence—without needing to understand crypto or alternative rails.

Merlin Use Case — USDC Checkout

1. Blocked Checkout

Consumer finds items cannot be purchased with a credit card.

2. Merlin Option

Merchant’s Merlin-enabled cart offers “Pay with USDC”.

3. Wallet & Compliance

Prism Managed Wallet activates and checks rules. Purchase < $1000 → no KYC required. Profile shows status transparently.

4. Invoice in USDC

Invoice updates to show all items in USDC. User clicks “Ready to Pay”.

5. Payment Window

QR code appears. User pays directly from their wallet.

6. USDC Store (if needed)

Not enough balance? Merlin routes to USDC Store to top-up with a credit card.

7. Settlement & Proof

DefiPay settles funds, verifies on blockchain, issues proof of purchase, and handles splits/commissions automatically.
