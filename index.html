What is the Prism Platform?

A governed platform for loyalty commerce

The Prism Platform is the business and technology layer that lets programs treat value like real money: blending points, cards, and approved rails into a single, compliant payment experience. Built for bank-grade scale, designed to extend into new channels without losing brand control.

Payment orchestration
