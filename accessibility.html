Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: [Insert Date]

Axxent Partners, Inc. is committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of technology or ability. We are actively working to increase the accessibility and usability of our site and in doing so adhere to many of the available standards and guidelines.

Standards We Follow

  • We aim to conform to WCAG 2.2 Level AA guidelines.
  • We follow relevant best practices from WAI-ARIA for interactive components.
  • We test with keyboard-only navigation and common screen readers where feasible.

What We’ve Implemented

  • Consistent structure: Semantic HTML for headings, landmarks, and lists.
  • Keyboard support: Navigable menus, modals, and controls with visible focus indicators.
  • Color & contrast: Text and interactive elements meet or exceed AA contrast ratios.
  • Images & media: Informative images include alt text; decorative images are marked accordingly. Videos include captions where provided.
  • Forms: Associated labels, clear error messaging, and helpful instructions.
  • Links & buttons: Meaningful names and accessible names for icons.
  • Responsive design: Content reflows without loss of functionality on small screens or zoom up to 200%.
  • Skip links: A “Skip to content” link is provided to bypass repetitive navigation.

Assistive Technology & Browser Support

We strive to support current versions of major browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) and common assistive technologies (for example, VoiceOver, NVDA). Performance may vary depending on your system configuration.

Third‑Party Content

Our site may include third‑party integrations (e.g., embedded videos, analytics, payment widgets). While we encourage our partners to meet accessibility standards, some third‑party content may be outside our control.

Known Limitations

We are aware of the following items and are working to improve them:

  • Certain legacy media assets may not yet include full captions or transcripts.
  • Occasional non‑critical contrast variances in archived pages.
