Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Axxent’s Universal Value platform, solutions, and integrations.

What is Universal Value?
Universal Value is our governed layer that lets customers redeem loyalty points, miles, gift balances, and brand credit like cash—online or in-store. It bridges traditional rewards and modern payment rails without requiring new hardware or a rip-and-replace.
How do merchants integrate?
You can start with hosted checkout pages, embed individual components like split-pay or wallet payouts, or go fully headless with our APIs. Most merchants start hosted and add components as they scale.
What payment methods are supported?
Customers can redeem points, miles, gift balances, and brand credit, and combine them with cards, wallets, or bank transfers. Merchants can settle in their preferred rails with clear reconciliation.
Does this work with Apple®?
Yes. Axxent supports Apple® rewards programs as well as a broad set of other issuers and brand currencies. The first mention per page is styled with the trademark, and the footer includes our Apple® disclaimer.
What about refunds and returns?
Refunds are handled through governed guardrails. If a customer used points + card, the refund respects program rules and settlement policies—keeping both the merchant and the customer whole.
Is the platform secure?
Yes. The platform is built with OAuth 2.0 / OIDC, scoped tokens, signed webhooks, and SLA’d support tiers. Security and compliance are covered in detail on our Security & Compliance page.
How do I get started?
Request sandbox access from our Developers page, or book a demo with our team. We’ll guide you from pilot to full rollout.
